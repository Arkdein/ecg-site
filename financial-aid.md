# Financial Aid

How else can I pay for university?
{: .fs-6 .fw-300 }

Cost should not stop you from going to university. There are four main sources of help. Amounts and income limits change, so follow the links for current figures.

## 1. Government help

- **[MOE Tuition Grant](https://www.moe.gov.sg/financial-matters/tuition-grant-scheme)** — pays a large share of tuition for eligible full-time courses. Singapore Citizens receive it automatically, with no bond. Permanent Residents and international students may apply, but must agree to work in Singapore for three years after graduating.
- **[Higher Education Community Bursary and Higher Education Bursary](https://www.moe.gov.sg/financial-matters/financial-assistance/financial-assistance-information-for-pseis)** — means-tested bursaries for Singapore Citizens at publicly funded universities, polytechnics and ITE, based on household income. MOE [enhanced these from AY2026](https://www.moe.gov.sg/news/press-releases/20251016-moe-financial-assistance-schemes-to-benefit-an-additional-31000-students).

## 2. CPF Education Loan Scheme

Parents (or you, or your spouse) can use **CPF Ordinary Account** savings to pay subsidised tuition fees for approved full-time courses at local institutions. It covers tuition only, not hostel or other fees, and the amount used is repaid to the CPF account after graduation. [How the scheme works](https://www.cpf.gov.sg/service/article/what-is-the-cpf-education-loan-scheme) · [Repayment calculator](https://www.cpf.gov.sg/member/tools-and-services/calculators/education-loan-repayment-calculator)

## 3. University financial aid

Each university has its own bursaries, loans and work-study schemes. Apply after you accept your offer.

[NUS](https://nus.edu.sg/oam/financial-aid/overview-eligibility/full-time-undergraduates) · [NTU](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/admissions/undergraduate/financial-matters/financial-aid/bursaries) · [SMU](https://admissions.smu.edu.sg/financial-aid/overview) · [SUTD](https://www.sutd.edu.sg/admissions/undergraduate/financing-options-and-aid/) · [SIT](https://www.singaporetech.edu.sg/admissions/financial-aid) · [SUSS](https://www.suss.edu.sg/admissions/financial-matters/financial-aid/full-time-undergraduate) · [University of the Arts Singapore](https://uas.edu.sg/admissions/scholarships-and-financial-assistance)

## 4. Work-study

Many universities offer paid campus jobs. They help with costs and build experience for your CV. Ask the university's financial aid or careers office once you enrol.
