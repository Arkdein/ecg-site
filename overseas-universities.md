# Overseas Universities

Is studying overseas for me, and where do I start?
{: .fs-6 .fw-300 }

Each destination has its own application system, timeline and costs, and some deadlines fall **before** you get your A-Level results. Start early, and talk to the teacher in charge of your destination.

| Destination | Apply through | Earliest deadline for 2027 entry |
|---|---|---|
| [China](china.md) | Each university directly | Varies by university; some open from late Sep 2026 |
| [United Kingdom](united-kingdom.md) | UCAS | 15 Oct 2026 (Oxford, Cambridge, medicine) |
| [United States](united-states.md) | Common App (most colleges) | Early rounds usually close in early to mid-Nov 2026 |

Paying for it: many overseas options have their own scholarships. See [Scholarships](scholarships.md) and the destination pages.

Thinking of somewhere else, such as Australia, New Zealand, Europe or Canada? [Talk to the ECG team](get-help.md).
