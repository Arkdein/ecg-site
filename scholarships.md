## Before you apply: is a bond right for you?

Many scholarships, especially from the government and companies, come with a **bond**: you agree to work for the sponsor for a number of years after graduating. Ask yourself:

1. Would I want to work for this organisation **even without the scholarship**?
2. What will the job actually involve day to day, and have I spoken to someone doing it?
3. How long is the bond, and where will I be in my life when it ends?
4. What happens if I change my mind about my course or career? What does breaking the bond cost?
5. Am I choosing this for the work, or for the prestige?

## What providers look for

Grades matter, but providers also look for **passion** for the field and genuine wish to serve, **confidence and composure** under questioning, **teamwork** (shown through CCA and projects), **analytical thinking**, and **foresight** — thinking several steps ahead.

<details class="ecg-level" markdown="1">
<summary>Common essay and interview prompts</summary>

- Tell us about yourself. What makes you different?
- Why do you want to study this field? What are your career goals?
- How do your strengths and goals fit this scholarship?
- How have you contributed to your community? What has your CCA taught you?
- Tell us about a time you failed, or a belief of yours was challenged. What did you learn?
- Why do you deserve this scholarship, and how will it help you?
</details>

<details class="ecg-level" markdown="1">
<summary>Three tips for essays and interviews</summary>

1. **Link your passions to the scholarship.** Show what drives you, and keep it relevant to what the provider does.
2. **Show your grit.** A setback you overcame, and what it taught you, says more than a list of awards.
3. **Share your plans.** Explain what you'll do with the opportunity and how you hope to contribute.
</details>

## Scholarships by sector

| Sector | Scholarship | Typical bond |
|---|---|---|
| Public service | [PSC Scholarships](https://www.psc.gov.sg/scholarships/undergraduate-scholarships/psc-scholarships) | Yes |
| Education | [MOE Teaching Scholarships](https://www.moe.gov.sg/careers/teaching-scholarships-sponsorships) | Yes |
| Healthcare | [Healthcare scholarships (MOH Holdings)](https://www.healthcarescholarships.sg/) | Yes |
| Defence, engineering and technology | [MINDEF scholarships](https://www.mindef.gov.sg/join-us/scholarships-and-sponsorships/scholarships/) (SAF, DSTA, DSO, CSIT) | Yes |
| Home Team | [MHA scholarships](https://www.mha.gov.sg/careers/mha-scholarships) | Yes |
| Community and youth | [People's Association scholarships](https://www.pa.gov.sg/careers/pa-scholarship/pa-scholarship/) | Yes |
| Digital, tech and media | [SG Digital Scholarship (IMDA)](https://imda.gov.sg/Singapore-Digital-Scholarship) | Yes |
| Studying in China | [SFCCA Scholarship](https://sfcca.sg/en/scholarship-bursary/) — for top Chinese universities | No |
| Sciences and humanities | [Loke Cheng-Kim Foundation](https://www.lokefoundation.com.sg/) | No |

Bond lengths vary with the course and where you study, commonly four to six years for government scholarships. Check each provider's terms.

### University scholarships

Most university scholarships have **no bond** for Singapore Citizens. Apply through the university, usually alongside your admission application.

[NUS](https://nus.edu.sg/oam/scholarships/scholarships-for-freshmen-singapore-citizens) · [NTU](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/admissions/undergraduate/scholarships-and-awards) · [SMU](https://admissions.smu.edu.sg/scholarships) · [SUTD](https://www.sutd.edu.sg/admissions/undergraduate/scholarship/sutd-administered/) · [SIT](https://singaporetech.edu.sg/undergrad/scholarships) · [SUSS](https://www.suss.edu.sg/admissions/financial-matters/scholarships-sponsorships/full-time-undergraduate) · [University of the Arts Singapore](https://uas.edu.sg/admissions/scholarships-and-financial-assistance)

### Comparing scholarships

[BrightSparks comparison tool](https://brightsparks.com.sg/compareScholarships.php) · [Scholars' Choice comparison](https://www.scholarschoice.com.sg/comparison/). Third-party sites can lag behind, so confirm details on the provider's own page.
