# My ECG Journey

What happens at my level, and what should I do this year?
{: .fs-6 .fw-300 }

Our aim is for every RV student to become a future-ready learner with a personal compass guided by purpose and passion. ECG at RVHS builds the self-awareness, skills and resilience to explore, plan and manage your own education and career path, and to value every kind of work.

The **RVHS ECG Framework** has three stages:

| Stage | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Awareness** — *Discover your purpose* | Understanding yourself: your values, interests, personality and strengths, and building your personal beliefs |
| **Exploration** — *Explore possibilities* | Discovering your interests, aspirations and passions, and building strengths and skills such as networking |
| **Planning** — *Plan your path* | Deepening your interests and skills, then deciding on and acting on your education and career plans |

## Your tools

- **[MySkillsFuture for Students](https://www.myskillsfuture.gov.sg/content/student/en/myskillsfuture-for-students.html)** — MOE's portal for self-assessments, and for exploring courses and careers. [User guides](https://www.myskillsfuture.gov.sg/content/student/en/preu/help/user-guides.html)
- **ECG e-portfolio** — where you record your reflections. Ask your form or civics tutor to help you set it up.
- **[MOE CourseFinder](https://www.moe.gov.sg/coursefinder)** — search post-secondary and university courses.

## Secondary
{: #secondary }

<details class="ecg-level" markdown="1">
<summary>Secondary 1 — Awareness</summary>

- **This year's programmes:** *Pathfinder Portfolios* in your RvX (blended learning) lessons, where you build a digital portfolio about yourself: who you are, what you value, what you've achieved and what you'd like to try next
- **One question to reflect on (RVX):** *What am I most proud of this year, and why?*
- **One tool to try:** a MySkillsFuture self-assessment
</details>

<details class="ecg-level" markdown="1">
<summary>Secondary 2 — Awareness</summary>

- **This year's programmes:** *Day X: Exploring the World of Work*, after the end-of-year exams, with hands-on taster activities from different jobs. It comes just before you choose your Sec 3 subjects.
- **One question to reflect on (RVX):** *Which subjects do I enjoy or do well in, and what kinds of work could they lead to?*
- **One tool to try:** the career explorer on [MySkillsFuture for Students](https://www.myskillsfuture.gov.sg/content/student/en/myskillsfuture-for-students.html)
</details>

<details class="ecg-level" markdown="1">
<summary>Secondary 3 — Exploration</summary>

- **This year's programmes:** the *Networking Skills Workshop*, where you practise introducing yourself, asking good questions and following up politely with people whose work interests you
- **One question to reflect on (RVX):** *Who do I already know who could tell me about a job I'm curious about?*
- **One tool to try:** a short conversation with an adult whose job interests you. Prepare three questions beforehand and send a thank-you message afterwards.
</details>

<details class="ecg-level" markdown="1">
<summary>Secondary 4 — Exploration</summary>

- **This year's programmes:** [Sec 4 ECG Experience](programmes.md#sec-4-ecg-experience) and fireside chats; [Student Internship Programme](opportunities.md#the-rvhs-student-internship-programme)
- **One question to reflect on (RVX):** *How do my MBTI preferences show up in the way I study and work with others?*
- **One tool to try:** your MBTI workshop notes, read alongside the career explorer on MySkillsFuture
</details>
