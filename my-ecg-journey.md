# My ECG Journey

What happens at my level, and what should I do this year?
{: .fs-6 .fw-300 }

Our aim is for every RV student to become a future-ready learner with a personal compass guided by purpose and passion. ECG at RVHS builds the self-awareness, skills and resilience to explore, plan and manage your own education and career path, and to value every kind of work.

The **RVHS ECG Framework** has three stages:

| Stage | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Awareness** — *Discover your purpose* | Understanding yourself: your values, interests, personality and strengths, and building your personal beliefs |
| **Exploration** — *Explore possibilities* | Discovering your interests, aspirations and passions, and building strengths and skills such as networking |
| **Planning** — *Plan your path* | Deepening your interests and skills, then deciding on and acting on your education and career plans |

## Your tools

- **[MySkillsFuture for Students](https://www.myskillsfuture.gov.sg/content/student/en/myskillsfuture-for-students.html)** — MOE's portal for self-assessments, and for exploring courses and careers. [User guides](https://www.myskillsfuture.gov.sg/content/student/en/preu/help/user-guides.html)
- **ECG e-portfolio** — where you record your reflections. Ask your form or civics tutor to help you set it up.
- **[MOE CourseFinder](https://www.moe.gov.sg/coursefinder)** — search post-secondary and university courses.
