## Before you choose

Applications are the last step. First, be clear about what you're applying for and why. Ask yourself:

- **Which kind of place suits me?** A university, a polytechnic, an arts institution or an overseas university: which fits my goals and the way I learn?
- **Which courses could I take on with confidence?** Do I meet the subject prerequisites, and would I enjoy the modules for three or four years?
- **How would this course build on my strengths?** Look at the projects, internships and overseas options, not only the course title.
- **Have I done my research?** Have I read the course pages, talked to students or graduates, and been to an open house?

Work through these on [Career & Courses](career-and-courses.md#how-to-choose-a-course), which also has a weighted table for comparing options.
