China has more than a thousand universities offering an enormous range of courses, often at a reasonable cost and with generous scholarships. International students are a growing part of the system. RV students' Chinese gives them a real advantage.

{: .deadline }
> **2027 entry:** application windows for September 2027 are opening now and overlap with the local university window. For example, Tsinghua's Round 1 runs from 30 Sep to 20 Nov 2026 and Round 2 from 21 Nov 2026 to 28 Feb 2027. Check each university's page.

## Scholarships

- **[Chinese Government Scholarship (CSC)](https://www.campuschina.org)** — applications are made through Campus China, the official site.
- **[SFCCA Scholarship](https://sfcca.sg/en/scholarship-bursary/)** — from the Singapore Federation of Chinese Clan Associations, for outstanding students at top Chinese universities, to develop bilingual and bicultural talent. No bond.
- **University scholarships** — most universities offer their own; see each admissions site.
