# For Parents

What should I talk about with my child at this stage?
{: .fs-6 .fw-300 }

Many teenagers don't have a clear plan, and that's normal. Your conversations matter more than having the answers. A question like *"What do you want to be?"* can feel like pressure; questions about what they enjoy, what they're curious about and what kind of life they want often open more doors. ([More on asking the right questions](https://study.uq.edu.au/stories/questions-ask-teenager-about-future), University of Queensland)

## The changing world of work

Today's teenagers will enter a very different job market. These are useful starting points:

- [OECD: The State of Global Teenage Career Preparation](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/the-state-of-global-teenage-career-preparation_d5f8e3f2-en.html) (2025) — how well teenagers' career plans match the world of work, and what helps
