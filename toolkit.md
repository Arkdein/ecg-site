## Know yourself

- **[MySkillsFuture for Students](https://www.myskillsfuture.gov.sg/content/student/en/myskillsfuture-for-students.html)**: MOE's portal. Take the self-assessments, then explore the courses and careers they point you to. Not sure how? Read the [user guides](https://www.myskillsfuture.gov.sg/content/student/en/preu/help/user-guides.html).
- **MBTI (Myers-Briggs Type Indicator)**: you complete it in Sec 4 (and in JC1 if you joined RVHS after the O-Levels). Your type describes what you naturally prefer, not what you're able to do, and there are no right or wrong types. It can't choose a career for you, but it gives you words for how you communicate, study and handle stress, and for working with people who differ from you. [More on MBTI at the Sec 4 ECG Experience](https://sites.google.com/moe.edu.sg/sec4ecgexperience/home)
